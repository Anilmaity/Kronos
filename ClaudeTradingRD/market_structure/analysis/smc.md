## Framework recap (one paragraph)

Price is engineered to move **from liquidity voids (FVGs/imbalance) toward liquidity pools (BSL above highs, SSL below lows)**. Every high holds buy-side liquidity (BSL: short stops + breakout buys); every low holds sell-side liquidity (SSL: long stops + breakdown sells). Smart money's footprint is a repeating rhythm: **sweep liquidity → form an Algo Candle (takes liquidity + leaves an FVG) → displace and break structure → retrace to the Order Block / FVG → continue.** A trend prints **HH/HL** (bullish) confirmed by **BOS** (break of structure = continuation); the first **CHoCH** (change of character = break of the most recent HL in an uptrend, or HH in a downtrend) signals the regime flip to **LH/LL**. Real structure breaks require price to first reach **discount** (for a bullish break) or **premium** (for a bearish break) of the dominant dealing range; equilibrium = 50%. **SMT divergence** (gold making a new high while correlated silver fails, or vice-versa) confirms exhaustion at the turn. All claims below are anchored to date + price from the daily/H4 CSVs and zigzag pivots.

## Cycles identified (SMC lens) — the repeating rhythm

The same four-beat cycle repeats at every turn; only the scale changes:

**SWEEP (take liquidity) → ALGO CANDLE (take + leave FVG) → DISPLACE / BOS or CHoCH → RETRACE to OB/FVG → CONTINUE.**

| Cycle | Sweep | Algo Candle / displacement | Structure event | Retrace target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seg A markup | SSL at 2615 (01-06) | laddered bull FVGs | repeated BOS up | 02-28 OB (2833) held → continuation |
| Seg B markup | SSL at 4022 / 4274 | 01-22 & 01-28 vectors | BOS up to 5602 | 12-31 OB (4274) |
| **Seg B top** | **BSL raid at 5602 (01-28)** | **01-28 Algo Candle (→5602, leaves 4831–5097 FVG)** | **CHoCH 01-30** | 5602 bearish OB defended |
| Seg B markdown | SSL sweeps 4402→4099 | 01-29→02-01 & 03-17 bear FVGs | BOS down (02-02, 03-23, 05-18) | 5420 / 4892 LH OBs |

**How runs cluster:** liquidity is harvested in **stair-step clusters** — equal highs at ~5191/5206 and ~4831/4858 were swept in single thrusts (03-02 ran the 5191/5206 EQH; April bear-flags ran the 4831 EQH). On the downside the **4480–4510 equal-lows shelf** (May) is the freshest unswept SSL magnet. Price consistently runs the **nearest stacked pool, then reverses from the OB/FVG behind it** — the engine never changes, only the direction of the dominant draw (BSL-seeking in markup, SSL-seeking in markdown).

## Reasoning / WHY — what was smart money doing

- **Segment A (markup):** Accumulation completed at the 2615 Strong Low; the algo then delivered a protected one-way markup, refusing to fill the laddered bullish FVGs because it was defending longs. The single EQ retrace (02-28, 2833) was a controlled re-load, not distribution — silver confirmed every high, so there was no exhaustion signal. Macro tailwind: DXY fell 109→104 and TNX eased 4.57→4.26 over the window, a clean weak-dollar / falling-yield bid for gold.
- **Segment B top (01-28, 5602):** This was a **premium BSL raid disguised as a breakout.** Gold rocketed +8.3% in a single daily candle into deep premium (>75% of the 4022–5602 range) to run every short-stop and breakout-buy above the prior highs — the Algo Candle. Smart money **distributed into the euphoria**: the 01-28→01-29 **bearish SMT** (gold failing to make a new high while silver squeezed to its own blow-off 121.67) is the fingerprint of institutions selling gold to retail FOMO buyers while silver's final spasm masked it. Macro context fits a blow-off, not a fundamental top: DXY at a cycle low (96.4) and VIX compressed to 16.4 on 01-28 — maximum complacency precedes the raid.
- **Segment B markdown (01-30 → May):** Once the 5097 inducement broke (CHoCH), delivery flipped to SSL-seeking. Every counter-rally (5092, 5420, 4892, 4774) was a **lower high rejected at the 4831–5097 bear FVG or the 5420 OB** — the algo re-loading shorts at premium and re-delivering into discount (4402 → 4099 → 4480). Silver's −38% single-day collapse on 01-30 corroborates a synchronized cross-asset distribution. The 4022 origin SSL was never breached (low 4099), so the markdown is, structurally, an incomplete leg — 4099 and the 4480–4510 shelf are the live draws.

## Confidence & caveats

- **~9 of 16 months only.** The 233-day gap (2025-04 → 2025-11) means **no continuity** between segments; the apparent jump 3098 → 4077 is data, not structure. Never connect A and B.
- **Daily/H4 OB & FVG zones are approximate.** Identification used daily 3-candle FVGs and last-opposing-candle OBs; **true precision needs M15/M5** drill-down (an H4 RB = LTF Algo Candle). H4 was used to refine the 5602 top (AC isolated to 01-28 20:00) and the CHoCH trigger (01-29 12:00), but intra-candle sequencing inside the daily blow-off is inferred.
- **SMT nuance:** gold and silver made *confirming* highs into 01-28; the bearish divergence is a **one-day desync (01-29)** — robust as a turn signal but tighter than a multi-day textbook divergence. Silver's 01-30 −38% candle may carry feed/rollover artifacts.
- **'ticks' = volume proxy**, not true exchange volume; used only directionally.
- Segment B structure read is **high confidence** (17 primary pivots, dense data); Segment A is a **single clean leg** — fewer structural events, so its OB/FVG inventory is necessarily thin (a trend doesn't build traps).
