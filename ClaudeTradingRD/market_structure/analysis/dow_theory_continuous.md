---

## 1. Framework recap

Dow Theory holds that (1) the averages discount all known information; (2) the market moves in three trends — a multi-month **primary** tide, multi-week **secondary** reactions against it, and day-to-day **minor** ripples; (3) the primary trend unfolds in three psychological **phases** — accumulation, public participation, distribution; (4) **the averages must confirm** one another (here: gold's advance must be ratified by silver); (5) **volume confirms the trend** — expanding with the tide, contracting on reactions; and (6) **a trend is presumed intact until a definitive reversal** is signalled by the price-pivot sequence (a primary bull is broken only when a secondary reaction undercuts a prior important reaction low AND the next rally fails to make a new high). This study reads the whole 432-bar continuous series as ONE regime.

## 5. Volume read ("volume confirms the trend")

The 'ticks' field saturates near ~66k on every active session (193 of 432 days ≥64k), so it cannot show expansion/contraction within the bull — it is effectively a binary "active vs. weekend" flag. The usable, unclipped activity/conviction gauge is **daily true range (% of price)**:

- **Markup reactions were quiet, the tide was active.** Through accumulation/markup, ranges stayed modest (mean leg range 32–65 pts) and reactions did not produce outsized days — consistent with "volume/activity expands with the trend, contracts on reactions."
- **Climax at the top is unmistakable.** The 12 widest-range days of the entire 16 months are ALL clustered at the top/markdown: **2026-01-30 (15.8% range — the single widest day), 02-02 (10.1%), 01-29 (9.2%), 03-23 (9.3%), 01-28 (8.1%)**. Range exploded only as the trend ended — the hallmark of a distribution/climax, not a healthy trend.
- **Activity confirmed the correction's first leg** (huge ranges Jan-30/Feb-02) then **dried up into the recovery** (R8/R9 ranges back to ~90 pts), which is *corrective* behaviour, not impulsive new-trend selling.

Verdict: volume/range **confirmed the bull throughout and flagged the top with a classic climax**, then failed to expand on the down-leg recovery — consistent with a correction, not a powerful new primary bear.

## 6. Reversal analysis — is the bull intact or reversed?

**Dow's definitive primary-reversal test (for a bull): a secondary reaction must close below a previous important secondary reaction low, AND the subsequent rally must fail to exceed the prior high.** Apply it:

Post-top primary sequence (price/kind):

| Date | Price | Kind | Relation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-01-28 | 5602 | H | THE TOP |
| 2026-02-02 | 4402 | L | first reaction low |
| 2026-03-02 | 5420 | H | **LOWER HIGH** (vs 5602) |
| 2026-03-23 | 4099 | L | **LOWER LOW** (vs 4402) → first confirmed bear signal |
| 2026-04-17 | 4892 | H | LOWER HIGH (vs 5420) |
| 2026-05-04 | 4501 | L | **HIGHER LOW** (vs 4099) ← bear sequence interrupted |

| 2026-05-20 | 4453 | L | lower low vs 4501 (minor) |

**The bearish trigger fired once: at 2026-03-23, the 4099 low closed below the 4402 reaction low while 5420 (03-02) was a lower high — a textbook Dow primary-reversal signal.** BUT two facts argue the bull is *not yet definitively reversed at the largest degree*:

1. **The long-term higher-low chain is unbroken.** 4099 (03-23) is still *above* the last pre-top important reaction low **3886 (2025-10-28)**. The bull's defining ascending-low line (3886 → 4274 → …) has not been violated.
2. **The market refused to extend the bear.** After 4099 it built a **higher low at 4501 (05-04)** and is hovering exactly on its 200-day MA (close 4510 vs MA200 4546, −0.8%). The down-sequence stalled rather than accelerating.

**Verdict: the primary trend has turned DOWN at the intermediate degree (a confirmed secondary→primary correction with one valid 03-23 reversal signal), but the 16-month primary BULL is NOT yet conclusively reversed.** It sits in Dow's classic *indeterminate "line"* between trends. 

- **Bull-intact confirmation would require:** a close back above the 5420 lower high (03-02), decisively above the all-time-high zone.
- **Confirmed primary BEAR would require:** a secondary close below **4099 (03-23)**, and ideally below **3886** — neither has occurred. The most recent low 4453 (05-20) is well above 4099.

Lean: **correction within a (stalling) bull**, with the burden of proof shifting toward the bears given the lower-high cascade 5602 → 5420 → 4892 → 4774 and the rising-yield headwind (§8).

## 7. Cycles identified (Dow lens)

Reading only the markup→reaction cadence of the primary path:

- **Rhythm of the bull:** advance legs ran **15–113 days** (median ~28–30d) and gained **+11% to +35%**; reactions ran **5–23 days** and gave back **−6% to −11%**. The repeating unit through the healthy bull was roughly *"~one-month markup of +15–30%, then a ~1–2 week reaction of −6 to −11%."* The three deepest-percentage advances (+35% Jun–Oct, +31% Dec–Jan, +21% Jan–Apr) bracket the trend.
- **Phase durations:** Accumulation ~6 months (Jan–Jun 2025), Public participation ~6 months (Jun–Dec 2025), Distribution/blow-off compressed into ~1 month (late Dec 2025–Feb 2026). The terminal phase *accelerated and shortened* — the standard parabolic signature.
- **Post-top cadence inverted.** Since 5602 the rhythm flipped to *reaction-dominant*: ~3-week down-legs (R6 5d but −21%, R7 21d −24%) separated by failing ~25-day rallies that each top lower (5420 → 4892 → 4774). The spacing between successive lower highs (~30 and ~25 days) mirrors the bull's markup cadence in reverse — a symmetric "stair-step down" replacing the prior "stair-step up."
- **Reaction-depth escalation** is itself the cycle tell: retracements ran 38%→70%→63%→44%→42% (all bull-legal) and then jumped to **90%→130%** at the turn — the clean break in the rhythm that dated the regime change to late January 2026.
