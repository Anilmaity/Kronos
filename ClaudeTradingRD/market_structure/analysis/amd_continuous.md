## AMD recap (1 paragraph)

The algorithm does not move price on supply/demand — it moves price to **engineer liquidity**. Every HTF leg follows the same three-beat rhythm: **Accumulation** (a quiet range that loads positions and stacks BSL above / SSL below), **Manipulation** (a Judas swing that sweeps one side's stops to fund the move and induce retail the wrong way), then **Distribution** (the real displacement leg into the *opposite* liquidity pool, leaving FVGs behind). The whole 16-month bull is a **fractal stack of nested AMD cycles**: small daily/weekly cycles nest inside the four major swing-degree cycles, which together built the run from the 2615 discount to the **5602 deep-premium BSL raid of the entire range** (equilibrium 4108). That blow-off is the headline manipulation-above-range on the HTF — it cleared the all-bull BSL, then delivered a –21% markdown in 5 days straight back down to the master 50% equilibrium (4099 ≈ 4108), exactly where the algo's bullish/bearish intent rebalances.

## Reasoning / WHY — tying manipulation & distribution to macro

- **2025-04 tariff panic (VIX spike to 47, SSL raid @2957):** the deepest fear event in the series. The algo used the risk-off panic to flush SSL below the early-2025 base, then delivered the +18% leg to 3500 — *induce fear → harvest stops → reverse*. Haven bid funded the discount accumulation.
- **Summer melt-up to 4381 (TNX falls to 3.99, the 16-mo low):** falling real yields removed gold's opportunity cost. With the lowest yields and benign VIX (16–18), the longest, deepest accumulation base (3244–3439, 90 days) loaded for the single largest distribution leg (+35%). Low yields = the macro fuel for the expansion.
- **Blow-off to 5602 (DXY 96.4 — 16-month dollar low; VIX only 16.4):** a collapsing dollar + complacent volatility (VIX 13.6 at the Dec 4550 top) is the perfect cocktail for a buying climax. Price made a **deep-premium BSL raid of the entire bull range**, clearing all-time stops. **Silver SMT confirms distribution:** gold's 03-02 retest (5420) was a *lower high* vs 5602, while silver collapsed from 121.67 to a 96.43 lower high — bearish SMT divergence at the very top. This is the algo's three objectives executed at maximum scale: *induce* breakout buyers above 5000/5500, *create panic* on the –21% 5-day collapse, *hit stops* of every late long.
- **Markdown to 4099 (VIX 26, DXY bounce to 98.9, TNX rising 4.05→4.33):** the dollar rebound + a fear spike + rising yields all flipped from tailwind to headwind simultaneously — the macro mirror image of the blow-off. Price delivered straight to **4099, ≈ the master 50% equilibrium (4108)**: the algo returned price to fair value after raiding the range top.
- **Current (4453, TNX 4.57 — highest in 16 mo, DXY 99.1):** rising yields are now the dominant headwind. Price sits at 62% of the master range (premium) in an unresolved corrective range — the most recent BSL raid @4774 (05-12) reversed to SSL @4453, money-transfer pointing lower near-term unless 4774/4891 BSL is reclaimed.

## Confidence & caveats

- **High confidence:** the AMD phase sequence, the master-range premium/discount math (5602 = 100% / deep-premium raid; 4099 ≈ 50% EQ within 9 pts), the displacement-leg sizing, and the sweep log are all directly computed from the daily/zigzag data — not eyeballed.
- **Medium confidence:** exact accumulation-range boundaries depend on the lookback window chosen (14-day rolling); the autumn (A4) and recent (A6) ranges are the fuzziest — A6 may yet resolve as distribution rather than re-accumulation.
- **Caveats:** (1) AMD is a *narrative framework* fitted post-hoc — the same data supports a simpler "Dow uptrend then correction" read; treat the cycle labels as interpretive, not predictive. (2) Daily bars hide intraday Judas timing (the true HTF manipulation often completes intraday). (3) The 2026-01-29 5097↔5595 intraday flip is blow-off noise; 5602 is THE peak. (4) Silver SMT and DXY inverse-correlation are confirming, not causal. (5) The series is a single (synthetic-continuous) bull regime — no bear market in-sample, so the markdown's depth/duration is the only "distribution-down" example and should not be over-generalized.
