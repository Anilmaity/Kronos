# Dow Theory Analysis — XAU/USD, 16-Month Study

*Classical Dow Theory only. No ICT/SMC vocabulary, no supply/demand zones. Every claim is anchored to a date and a price. The confirming average is **silver**; the "discounts everything" inputs are TNX (US10Y yield), DXY (dollar index) and VIX.*

---

## Framework recap

Dow Theory reads the market through six tenets: (1) the averages **discount everything**; (2) the market has **three trends** — Primary (months–years), Secondary (reactions, weeks), Minor (noise, days); (3) the primary trend unfolds in **three phases** — Accumulation, Public Participation / Markup, Distribution; (4) the **averages must confirm one another** (here gold vs silver); (5) **volume confirms the trend** (expands with the primary direction, contracts on reactions, climaxes at exhaustion); (6) a **trend persists until a definitive reversal** is signalled by a failure to extend plus a break of the prior reaction extreme. The zigzag scales map directly: **primary = 6% reversal**, **secondary = 3%**, **minor = 1.5%**.

**Data caveat up front:** the feed is split by a ~233-day hole (2025-04-01 .. 2025-11-19). Segment A and Segment B are treated as two independent Dow studies; no trend is read across the gap. The 'ticks' volume proxy is **saturated near ~65k** on most active days, so for the volume tenet I lean on **daily range expansion/contraction** as the more honest activity gauge, cross-checked against ticks.

## Reversal signals (exact dated triggers)

**Segment A:** No primary reversal. The single secondary reaction (02-24→02-28, −4.2%) was bought; higher highs persisted to 03-30. Trend remained UP through end of data.

**Segment B — primary up → down, staged:**
1. **2026-01-28 — blow-off climax / exhaustion.** 5602 peak on a 445-pt range bar, followed by 499/772-pt range bars (01-29/01-30). Climactic volatility = first warning, not yet a reversal.
2. **2026-02-02 — abnormal reaction depth.** −21.4% (≈90% retrace of the prior leg) far exceeds Dow's 2/3 norm; trend health damaged.
3. **2026-03-02 — confirmed LOWER HIGH (5420 < 5602).** Failure to make a higher high = first structural pillar of reversal.
4. **2026-03-19 — break of prior secondary low.** Day-low 4502 closes below the 02-06 reaction low (4655). First lower-low break.
5. **2026-03-23 — reversal CONFIRMED.** Price 4099 breaks below the 02-02 reaction low (4402); combined with the 03-02 lower high this is the complete Dow reversal signal (lower high **and** lower low). **The primary trend turned from UP to DOWN on 2026-03-23.**
6. **Silver corroboration:** silver's −23.5%-from-peak reading at gold's 03-02 lower high gave the reversal an *averages-non-confirmation* stamp days before gold's own price broke.
7. Post-reversal, the 04-17 high (4892) and 05-12 high (4774) are successively lower highs *inside* the bear — no re-reversal; trend remains DOWN at 4480 (05-18).

## Cycles identified (Dow lens)

- **Markup→reaction cadence (Seg B):** a repeating rhythm of a sharp markup leg followed by a ~6–9% secondary reaction. Bull-phase reactions retraced **~50–65%** of the prior leg (12-31, 02-04, 04-17); the moment reactions exceeded **100%** (03-19 onward) the primary had flipped. The retrace % itself is the regime tell.
- **Phase-duration rhythm:** both segments show a ~3–5 week accumulation, a ~3–4 week markup, then a multi-week reaction/distribution. Seg A accumulation ≈3 wks, markup ≈4 wks. Seg B accumulation ≈5 wks (11-21→12-26), blow-off markup ≈4 wks (12-31→01-28), distribution ≈5 wks (01-28→03-02), markdown leg ≈3 wks (03-02→03-23).
- **Climax→distribution→markdown sequence:** Seg B is a clean Dow life-cycle — accumulation → public mania (parabola) → volatility-climax top → multi-week distribution with a lower high → markdown. This is the repeating archetype to watch for: *parabola + range climax + lower high = top*.
- **Reaction-volatility expands at turns:** in both segments daily range peaks **at** the reaction/top, not in the trend body (Seg A 48.5 at the 02-28 reaction; Seg B 466 at the 01-28 top) — turning points are high-energy, trend bodies are calmer.

## Reasoning / WHY — turns tied to macro ("averages discount everything")

- **Seg A bull (2615→3098):** drove on a **falling dollar and easing yields** — DXY **108.3 → 104.0**, TNX **4.62 → 4.26** across the leg, while VIX crept **16 → 21.6** (rising risk anxiety bid gold as a haven). Gold discounted dollar weakness + creeping fear in real time.
- **Seg B accumulation (4022, 11-21):** struck at **VIX 23.4** (peak fear) with DXY already soft (100.2) — Dow's accumulation phase always begins in disbelief; the macro fear backdrop fits.
- **Seg B blow-off to 5602 (01-28):** coincided with **DXY collapsing to 96.4** (16-month-low dollar) — a currency-debasement / haven mania. The parabola *discounted* the weak dollar to an extreme.
- **The reversal's macro driver:** from the 01-28 top, **TNX backed up sharply — 4.25 (01-28) → 4.33 (03-23) → 4.45 (05-04) → 4.62 (05-18)**, and DXY stabilised/firmed (96.4 → ~99). **Rising real yields + a firming dollar removed gold's two props**, exactly as the primary trend rolled over. VIX spiking to **26.1 (03-23)** marks the liquidation low. The averages discounted the yield turn *before* the price reversal completed.
- **Silver's role:** as the higher-beta monetary metal, silver amplified both moves — confirming the mania top to the day, then leading the markdown (−37% vs gold −19%) as the same yield/dollar headwinds hit the more speculative average first.

## Confidence & caveats

- **Coverage:** only ~9 of 16 calendar months carry data; a ~233-day hole (2025-04 .. 2025-11) means **no primary trend can be traced across the gap**. Conclusions are intra-segment only.
- **Seg A is short (12 weeks):** it captures only one primary up-leg with no top — its distribution phase is simply absent from the window, so "no reversal" means "none observed," not "none possible."
- **Volume tenet is soft:** the 'ticks' proxy saturates near ~65k, so the volume-confirms-trend conclusions rest mainly on **daily-range** behaviour; treat the range-as-volume substitution as a reasoned proxy, not true traded volume.
- **Intraday noise:** the 01-29 same-day 5097↔5596 flip is blow-off noise; 5602 (01-28) is treated as THE peak per the backbone.
- **Confidence:** **High** on the Seg B reversal call (lower high 03-02 + lower-low break 03-23, silver non-confirmation, yield back-up all align). **High** on Seg A bull + silver confirmation. **Moderate** on phase boundary dates (±a few sessions) and on volume-climax magnitude given tick saturation.
