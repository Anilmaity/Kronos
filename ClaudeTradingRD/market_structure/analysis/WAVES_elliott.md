# XAU/USD — Elliott Wave restructure (continuous 16 months)

The same continuous data, re-expressed as a wave count: **trends move in 5 waves
(impulse), extending to 9 when a wave extends; corrections in 3 (A-B-C).** Gold's
16-month bull is a clean **5-wave Primary impulse with an extended 5th** (→ 9 swings),
followed by an **A-B-C flat** correction.

## 2. Why "5 → 9": the extended 5th

Wave (5) is the longest wave (W5/W1 = 1.94, W5/W3 = 1.36) → a **fifth-wave extension**,
typical of commodity blow-off tops. An extension makes the 5th subdivide visibly into its
own five sub-waves:

| Sub | From → To | Dates |
|-----|-----------|-------|
| i | 3886 → 4550 | 10-28 → 12-26 |
| ii | 4550 → 4274 | 12-26 → 12-31 |
| iii | 4274 → 5191 | 12-31 → 01-27 |
| iv | 5191 → 5014 | 01-27 |
| v | 5014 → 5602 | 01-27 → 01-28 (final blow-off) |

So the complete impulse displays as **9 swings** = waves (1),(2),(3),(4) + the five
sub-waves of the extended (5). That is exactly the "mostly 5, sometimes 9" you described.

- High confidence: the 5-wave impulse + rule compliance + the 50%/4108 confluence.
- Medium: the extended-5th sub-labels (i–v) are daily-resolution; the **current** position

  eliminate them. Treated as one lens among four, not a standalone forecast.
