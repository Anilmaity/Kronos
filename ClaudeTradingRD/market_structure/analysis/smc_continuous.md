## 1. SMC recap

Price is delivered by an algorithm that engineers liquidity (stops/breakout orders clustered above highs = **BSL**, below lows = **SSL**), then **displaces** — leaving **FVGs** (inefficiencies it must rebalance) and **Order Blocks** (the last opposing candle before the impulse). A run that takes liquidity and then **breaks structure** in the new direction is a **BOS** (continuation) when it agrees with the prevailing draw, or a **CHoCH** (change of character) when it breaks the *opposite-side* swing for the first time, signalling intent has flipped. **Inducement** is minor liquidity placed just in front of a real level to bait early entries. **Premium/discount** frames the whole dealing range around its **equilibrium** (50%): smart money accumulates in discount, distributes in premium, and price returns to equilibrium as fair value. Over these 16 months gold ran one enormous discount-to-premium delivery (2615 → 5602), distributed at 5602 with a silver **SMT divergence**, and is now marking down — having already returned price to the exact 4108 equilibrium at the 4099 low.

## 9. Cycles identified (SMC lens)

The 16 months are one repeating four-beat rhythm at every degree — **sweep → displacement(BOS) → retrace-to-OB/FVG → continue**:

1. **Liquidity run:** price sweeps an SSL (discount low) or a minor inducement. *Ex:* 3201 swept (05-01) below the obvious low.
2. **Displacement / BOS:** a vector candle breaks the prior swing high, leaving an FVG. *Ex:* 3244→3500 BOS↑ leaving the 3252–3323 bull FVG.
3. **Retrace to OB/FVG:** price returns to rebalance the FVG / mitigate the OB. *Ex:* the 4012–4097 Nov FVG re-tested before 4550.
4. **Continuation to next BSL:** delivery resumes toward the next high. *Ex:* on to 4381, then 4550, then 5602.

**Liquidity runs cluster** at the range extremes: the *discount* SSL ladder (2957/3121/3244/3886) was accumulated and **never revisited** (strong-trend order protection), while the *premium* BSL (5602) was the singular terminal draw. After the top the same rhythm **inverts** to a bearish cycle: BSL grab at 5602 → displacement down (01-30 bear FVG) → retrace to fill that FVG at 5420 → continuation down to the 4099 equilibrium. The markdown is one clean **bearish money-transfer**: BSL delivery (toward 5602) flipped to SSL delivery (toward 4099/4108 EQ) at the 01-29 CHoCH.
