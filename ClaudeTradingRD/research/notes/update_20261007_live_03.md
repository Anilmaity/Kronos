### Rules stated in Q&A (mechanical where possible)
- **C2 relabel**: if C3 opens and sweeps the C2 extreme, that candle is now C2; wait for a new closure.
- **Candle counting**: Thursday C2 → Friday C3 → Monday C4; a bank-holiday Monday does not count.
- **Trading a C2 is a reversal trade** → more likely to fail than the next day's C3 continuation.
- **Large manipulation leg**: if the H1 CISD series spans most of the prior day, substitute a
  4H candle closure or a new continuation (close over a reached FVG) — while noting the trend
  mechanically has not shifted until the CISD close.
- **Structural SMT** (at range extremes) vs swing-point SMT; structural may be read before the
  model closes. SMT confirmed by a CISD. Trade the weakest asset short / strongest long.
- **CISD vs OB**: a CISD is the order block that changes the trend; same-direction ones are
  continuation OBs.
- **Soft invalidation**: bearish bias + H1 close above daily C2 EQ → larger wick, less expansion;
  targets cut to daily open / intraday lows / sometimes PDL.
- **One-sided test**: can you frame the market both ways? If yes it is confusing — skip.
  Learners should focus on C3 daily candles. Even an "ideal" C3 closure is downgraded if it has
  already expanded through the intraday low.
- **C3 heading into an imbalance**: judge by bias strength and phases of price at that level.
- **Macros fake**: a macro is ~10 min either side of an HTF candle open; wait for the new candle.
  9:30 is the stock-market open, not a macro.
- **Partials math**: 2R break-even WR 34%; exiting at 1R → 50%.
- **Autobias 1/2** explained (H4/M15 resp. D1/H1 agreement for an H1/M5 model); no tiebreak
  rule when they disagree.
- **Indicator**: XC2 = failed C2 (non-repainting); dots = protected swings; "quiz template" drill.
- **Swing**: daily chart, enter daily or aligned hourly; monthly+daily, refine H4/H1; no SD targets.
- **Frequency**: 0–1 trades/day with D/H4/M15; a week or two without a setup is normal.
- **Long-term**: buy below the yearly open after the wick forms; no "discount" in a trend.
- **C4**: he claims two defined ways to trade C4 when C3 doesn't expand — deliberately not taught.
- Psychology: change or quit; take the loss, feel it, don't do dumb stuff; no signals; define
  model + session to avoid chart-watching.
- Data: uses CME futures (GC1/COMEX), not CFDs; doesn't use COT, EMAs, order flow, OTE,
  midnight open as EQ, breaker blocks (prefers the gap).
