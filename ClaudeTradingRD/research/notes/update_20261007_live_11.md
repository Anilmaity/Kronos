## New York Open Live Q&A  (KBJAGgkXdeI, https://youtu.be/KBJAGgkXdeI, 3217s)

### What is actually taught
A short, low-conviction NY open stream (he says he almost did not stream; busy week). Three threads:

1. **Gold invalidation trade (the main idea).** Previous stream's plan: a daily candle 2 at a
   *potential low of the month*, retesting the area. Bullish only if an intraday confirmation and a
   continuation over the level formed. On the hourly no continuation formed, so the setup "failed" and
   the draw flips to the **previous day low**. He later replays it three minutes at a time: price
   **closed below the EQ and formed a bearish continuation** - that is the invalidation. He names the
   two invalidation routes explicitly: (a) reverse off the previous day high (pointing at NQ), or
   (b) fail the EQ (gold). Target "always" the previous day low. He frames C2 closure, C3 closure and
   their invalidation as the "easiest bias" because, unless it consolidates, a setup either works or
   fails - both give a trade. Refers viewers to his "trading the invalidation of daily bias" video.
2. **Indices: no bias.** NQ strongest, YM weakest pre-open, then YM takes strength. After four
   consecutive daily expansion days he expects consolidation or a retracement to a higher low (a fifth
   day acknowledged as possible), so no index bias. Protocol: wait 5, 15, 30 minutes after 9:30.
   A 15m reversal candle off the open on a day where expansion is anticipated is not traded - wait
   for 10:00. Notes the rare case of **ES leading** ("nine out of 10 days ES does not lead"), and
   that ES then trends and the others follow - which they did on screen.
3. **FX/DXY.** Dollar breaking higher toward a daily gap; frames GBP (preferred) and EUR shorts; hold
   until DXY hits the gap, or to the close on an expansion day.

### Rules stated
- With no bias: no entries; do not drop to lower timeframes to find one; go higher or stay out.
- "I don't pattern trade things" - an FVG print does not make him bearish.
- Continuation formed late in a 30m candle that has used its range and time -> wait for next 30m / 9:30.
- In the invalidation, enter only after the bearish continuation forms, not in the reversal candle;
  if price sweeps a new high, wait for a new C2.
- Bullish 3m candle against a short into a 3m FVG: let it close; next candle must react; small wick
  then trading lower can expand, too high a wick less likely.
- Hourly idea, 5m entry lacked R to the PDL without a limit order; he does not like a limit there, so
  3m/1m.
- Not a fan of 1m SMT to frame an idea; does not trust 3m SMT with silver.
- Below the PDL a 1m failure-to-manipulate + continuation is a trail-stop point, never a new entry.
- Never short the strongest asset (YM) when bearish; trade ES/NQ.
- No dollar-vs-indices correlation; "think less".
- C1 is arbitrary, always the candle before C2; C2 is always the high (bearish case).
- TTFM indicator: no FVG -> no protected swing printed on the 3m.
- Not a fan of breakouts without clean swings below; does not mind a breakout before range highs
  are taken (stated about DXY; not drafted - too vague).
