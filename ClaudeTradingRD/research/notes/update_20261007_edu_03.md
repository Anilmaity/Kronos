## How to Use Average Daily Range (ADR) with PDH / PDL — No More Fakeouts  (3OIq9HmckH8, https://youtu.be/3OIq9HmckH8, 963s)

### What is actually taught
The video answers one question: **after previous day high (PDH) or previous day low (PDL) is
taken, should you look for a continuation, or expect consolidation / retracement / reversal?**
PDH/PDL are framed as "the easiest draws on liquidity". The answer is a three-part gate:

1. **Remaining average daily range.** ADR is defined arithmetically: sum the high-to-low range
   of N daily candles and divide by N. His slide example: 297 + 379 + 314 = 990 points over three
   days -> ADR 330 points ("Ideally, you use a few more" than three). The ADR is laid on the
   current daily candle **measured from the day's extreme already made** (the high, for a bearish
   day). If, at the moment PDH/PDL is taken, most of the ADR is already used, he does not expect a
   continuation; if a lot of range is left, continuation is valid.
2. **An open target** beyond the PDH/PDL that sits inside the remaining ADR. A target "beyond
   reach" of the ADR can still be acceptable if price is clearly reaching for it.
3. **The higher-timeframe target is not already taken.** Even with enough ADR, if the day taking
   PDH/PDL is "candle four ... after we've had multiple days of expansion" and it also takes the
   HTF target, he does not expect a continuation — price "could consolidate, retrace, form a
   reversal". He ties this to "phases of price" and recognising "failure to manipulate" vs a
   "breakout signature" (concepts not defined here).

The mechanism linking it to wick size: a **large opposing move "is using time and range"** — when
the daily candle spends its range forming a big wick, the ADR projected from that wick's extreme
reaches only to the PDH/PDL, so PDH/PDL becomes the day's target and **not** something to trade
through; he waits "for the new candle or the new daily open" to get new range. A small wick
respecting the EQ / T-spot leaves the ADR free to reach a farther target.

Tooling: TradingView built-in "Average Daily Range" (under technicals), timeframe setting
changed from "chart" to **daily**. But in the same video he says "I like to eyeball the ADR".
No lookback length is stated for the indicator.

Entry-location addendum (one line): "I don't want to trade too high into the range because I want
to enter around the opening price early into the candle before targets are taken out."

### Worked chart examples (numbers as spoken; instrument names mostly not stated)
- Slide: three daily candles 297 / 379 / 314 -> ADR 330.
- TradingView ex. 1 (high-volatility index, ADR ~950, "over 900 points" ranges): high to equal lows
  = 600 points; price went ~730 high-to-low and took PDL -> "not really looking for too much
  expansion"; result consolidates, closes near lows.
- Next day: ADR ~880; PDH/PDL both "in range" (~600 / ~800 away). Price trades beyond PDH with ~800
  of range used -> wait for new daily candle; price consolidates.
- Next: PDH only 40-50 points away, ADR ~870, open highs to the left ~300 away -> continuation
  beyond PDH valid; later at 400-500 used, could see 700-800.
- ADR ~400 example: failure swings ~400 above as target; a large opposing move consumed range so
  the reachable target shrank to PDH; he would not trade long beyond PDH that day.
- ADR ~116 (C2/T-spot example): with a small wick the lows ~115 below would be the target; a large
  wick formed, so 115 from the high lands at PDL -> PDL reached, no continuation "Not until the
  next day".
- ADR ~100-105: PDL taken after only ~50 points of range -> continuation lower still valid
  ("we have not capped our daily range"). Then: PDH taken right away, still range left ->
  continuation valid until the ~105 is reached, then "we've capped out our daily range".

## Internal & External Liquidity Using the TTrades Fractal Model  (vMq1l8Zzzjw, https://youtu.be/vMq1l8Zzzjw, 857s)

### What is actually taught
Definitions (crisp, his own): **internal liquidity = a fair value gap** (3-candle pattern, wick of
C1 does not overlap wick of C3); **external liquidity = a high or low, a swing point** (a high with
a lower high each side). "The market will move between internal and external liquidity."

The fractal-model gate: the rotation is only tradeable when a **candle 2 (or candle 3) closure**
prints at the level — "Just because we have a fair value gap doesn't mean price is going to reach
into it". C2 closure at an FVG (internal) -> trade C3 (and C4) toward external; after external is
taken, a C2 closure there -> trade back into internal (FVG) — "not just blindly thinking it should
go to internal liquidity".

Target selection: when a reversal is shown at internal liquidity, the external target "is always
simply going to be the swing low that made the swing high" (or swing high that made the swing
low). He notes the example target is a failure swing in his "relevant swings" sense, but uses it.

Preference: "people have an easier time trading from fair value gaps inside the higher time frame
trend towards external liquidity" than trading the retracement back into the range.

Quality filter at the FVG: a very bullish candle closing with only a small wick at the gap is "not
very bearish or telling of a reversal" — he waits for the next candle rather than call a reversal.

Execution ladders demonstrated: daily -> hourly -> 5m; daily -> 4H -> 15m; weekly -> 4H -> 15m;
and 15m -> 1m. Entries on a new protected swing (close through after reaching into an FVG).
Targets: fixed 2R (the LTF target) **or** hold runners to the HTF external target ("if you want
more risk to reward"). Stop: on the swing high or "on the bodies" — and in the 4H/15m example he
puts it on the lower-timeframe high rather than the 4H high because "I want to see 50% of this
being respected, and my stop loss is still over 50% of that". Also: "stop trying to jam your stop
loss" — enter on LTF, target HTF.

During expansions he wants **shallow retracements**, not a return into premium.

### Worked chart examples
- Ex. 1 (daily, unnamed): FVG -> C2 closure -> C3 and C4 lower toward the swing low; then a
  wickless bullish candle into two FVGs — not read as a reversal until the next candle; it then
  reverses from both gaps toward external.
- Ex. 2 (daily + correlated asset): PDH taken, small wick, **SMT** vs correlated asset -> bearish
  bias; hourly: SMT, PSP, CISD, new swing at an hourly FVG (C2 closure). 5m model: new protected
  swing, stop on the high or the bodies, 2R ~ the LTF target; HTF target = daily low, hit "into the
  next day". Same idea via 4H/15m: stop on LTF high (still over 50%), TP hit.
- Ex. 3 (weekly): weekly FVG + weekly C2 closure -> weekly target previous low; 4H valid model
  (CISD, continuations, "intraweek high" via intra-candle CISD); 4H FVG reached shallowly, 4H C2
  closure -> 15m: CISD, then a new continuation where the T-spot overlaps an FVG, entry on the open
  of the new candle, 2R hit; held version reaches the HTF objective much later.
- 15m C2 closure at an FVG -> 15m/1m model toward external.

### Transcript hygiene
"candle to closure" = candle two closure (throughout); "change in the state delivery";
"inter-candle" not here but "intra candle"; "two R" = 2R; "T spot". Instruments unnamed.

## How to Trade Asia Using TTrades Fractal Model  (BgVkJf0kMo4, https://youtu.be/BgVkJf0kMo4, 680s)

### What is actually taught
**Only two situations** in which he trades Asia: (a) an **expected expansion candle** (daily C3
after a daily C2 closure — the focus of this video), or (b) the day **opens near a drawn
liquidity** so Asia can expand right off the daily open (stated, not demonstrated). A
higher-timeframe bias is mandatory: "when you are taking entries in Asia, you really do need a
bias".

Two execution methods for case (a):
1. **Positional entry (hourly)** — inside the daily C2 closure a protected swing has already
   formed on the hourly before candle 3 opens, and it is "very close in proximity to the open of
   candle three"; enter at/around the daily open, stop on that invalidation, expect the daily wick
   to form there, target 2R. Points back to his separate positional-entry video.
2. **4H + 15m fractal model (trading the daily wick)** — when the hourly structure "doesn't give me
   much confidence", wait for a **4H candle 2 closure**, then on the 15m trade the standard model
   on an intra-candle CISD / new protected swing. Ideal: the 4H closure is paired with an hourly
   CISD intraday ("if you've seen my intraday reversals video"). Stop on the 15m protected high,
   which in his example is "the wick high of the 4-hour candle"; target 2R, or look up targets on
   the hourly for more R.

"It doesn't have to occur in Asia" — these are the cases where Asia forms the high/low of the day.

**Advanced: trading candle 2 (reversal day) in Asia.** Sweep of PDL after a large expansion, small
wick, hourly CISD + SMT. "I obviously cannot just enter positionally" — reversal setup, so: hourly
confirmation first, **then** wait for a 4H candle closure (confirms C2), then a 15m new
continuation formed before the open of the next 4H candle; stop on that continuation's low; 2R
(which coincided with an indicator liquidity point). Reason for the extra layers: "it is more
risky and so I need more confirmations".

**Overnight management:** "I'm going to take my entry, go to bed" — stopped or TP, no active
management of overnight/Asia entries.

### Worked chart examples
- CAD futures (6C), monthly reversal candle -> bullish monthly bias; previous daily = ideal C2
  closure (into a daily FVG, close over the down-close candles) = daily protected swing / IC-CISD
  for the daily-monthly model. Hourly: CISD, protected swing, then a second protected swing close
  to the candle-3 open -> positional long around the open, stop on that swing, 2R hit.
- Ex. 2 (unnamed): daily C2 closure + hourly CISD; no POI so a high is used as POI; hourly
  positional rejected (structure poor) -> 4H C2 closure (respecting daily EQ; reaches a bit higher
  "cuz of this week closure"), hourly continuation also present -> 15m: wait for new IC-CISD at
  the EQ / T-spot / FVG, displacement lower, new protected swing, short, stop on the 4H wick high,
  2R hit (a further continuation formed en route, usable to trail or add).
- Ex. 3 (reversal day): PDL swept after large expansion, small wick, hourly CISD + SMT; 4H closure
  confirms C2; 15m new continuation before next 4H open; long, stop on continuation low, 2R ~
  indicator liquidity line; consolidation into New York before TP.

### Transcript hygiene
"candle to closure" = candle two closure; "inter-candle change" = intra-candle CISD; "this week
closure" probably "this wick closure" (ambiguous); "two R". No times given for Asia session
boundaries — "Asia" is never defined in clock terms.
