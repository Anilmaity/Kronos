### What is actually taught (TTrades)
1. **Pre-open bias (indices).** The index failed to close over the level he flagged the
   previous day → leans bearish, targets previous day low and a lower intraday low. NQ named
   weakest. ES took a high with SMT; "the stronger asset has closed through the series of
   up-close candles". For a bearish day he does not want the 7 a.m. high taken out. Plan:
   15m imbalance at the open as a sell area.
2. **Open: strength switch.** At 9:30 everyone in the competition went long; YM went weaker,
   NQ stronger ("is YM lying or is NQ lying?"). He focuses on ES while the ranking bounces.
3. **Weakest-asset reversal timing.** YM (weakest) reverses off its low; he wants a
   **5-minute closure over** to confirm. YM closes through, then NQ runs out its equal highs
   and the previous highs. He explains: wait for the weakest correlated market to reverse,
   because that is when the strongest starts to trend. → `cross-asset-reversal-confirmation`.
4. **Bias flip.** Bearish idea invalidated by a closure over the daily open (a "W bottom");
   then bias = reach the previous highs.
5. **Mechanical consolidation definition** (asked by chat): "price fails to take out a
   previous range". Yesterday was an inside day; today "by definition so far" also one.
   Typical response: avoid; if you must trade (competition), ask whether PDH or PDL is
   more likely and that is your bias. → `consolidation` (new rule for that entry).
6. **10:00 4-hour close.** New 4H candle closes strong (~305 NQ). After a far expansion that
   took the highs, he expects a new phase — retracement or consolidation — before any
   continuation to PDH.
7. **No sweep, no counter-frame.** ES and YM did not sweep the previous 4H high → "no way for
   me to frame shorts", trend stays bullish (higher highs, higher lows) until a reach above the
   previous 4H high or bearish price action. When ES does then sweep it with a small SMT, he
   still declines: he is a continuation trader; the only frame is a 15m swing / 1m reversal
   on the weaker asset, "not my style".
8. **Daily EQ / wick.** The daily EQ should be respected if the day expands with a small
   wick; it was not, the daily candle now has a large wick, so he expects little range today
   (while noting he usually does not fade the daily candle). → `small-wick-expansion-rule`.
9. **Failure to manipulate across assets.** Later YM continues through the 9:30 lows instead
   of reversing ("failing to manipulate this previous low"), and ES/NQ follow back toward the
   9:30 lows; 1m shows a gap stopping price, a candle-2 closure and a new protected swing.
10. **Other markets (top-down):** Gold — cannot frame longs until price runs the previous-day
    lows into the daily gap; a reversal before that is a failure swing (refers to his
    relevant-swings video). Later gold hits that low: no longer bearish, but he will not trade
    the bounce because it is a reversal. Oil — targets monthly open and a low; monthly candle
    is doji-like, so he expects it to close near its open. Dollar — his bearish C2 call failed.
    Bitcoin into month end — do not fade the trend; continuation would open low, wick, trade
    through and fail to manipulate the level; a reversal would come off the previous month's
    high.
11. **Q&A rules:** continuations over reversals because of higher win rate; SMT only after a
    valid model, as confluence; CISD need not appear on both assets; indicator patterns cannot
    be traded alone ("align with something"); he runs the indicator at default except early C2
    on, and without it a CISD prints only at HTF close; no C2 closure → C3 closure / OHLC /
    daily or intra-candle profile / phases of price; no HTF sweep → intraday profile or wait
    for next day; no midnight open (daily open only), quarterly theory "is fake", no Bookmap, no
    forex crosses/CFDs; uses daily, 4H, 1H, 30m, 15m (speaker of that line not certain).
