### Rules stated in Q&A (mechanical where possible)
- **Top-down for a 3m entry**: use the 30m for structure, bias from H4/daily.
- **Daily profile monitoring timeframe**: any timeframe can be used ("Still the daily
  candle"); M15 better for reversal days (H1 too slow), H1 works for continuation days.
- **Kill zones rejected**: he trades once the higher-timeframe wick has formed — the daily's
  wick and an intra-day H4/H1 wick — and needs a spot for invalidation. 9:30 volatility (ETF /
  stock open) is acknowledged as a reason activity concentrates there, not as a rule.
- **ADR "rope" analogy**: e.g. 500 points of NQ range per day is a rope. If Asia makes the low
  and London expands, little is expected in New York; if the overnight uses no range and New
  York reverses, New York is expected to use the rest. Refined: ask how much **range AND time**
  was used to form the day's wick — a quick wick leaves rope to expand, a wick that consumed
  most of the range/time leaves little. (ADR video and "after previous day high/low taken"
  video announced as forthcoming.)
- **Trailing-drawdown sizing**: with $1,500 trailing drawdown, risking $700 and making
  $1,500 then returning to break-even ends the account; risking $100 leaves $1,400. "risk less
  and use more accounts if you want to get leverage."
- **Failure swing (vs The Strat)**: answering a Strat trader — a failure swing is not a "failed
  2"; it is "just all those highs that are lined up right without taking it out" (≈ the Strat
  trader's "pivot machine gun").
- **No bias → demand better LTF structure**; "Are you actually willing to put money on the
  market on a day like this, even if you do find an entry that follows your system?"
- **Negative R:R**: works for some, fine for prop-firm math, but he prefers a structure where
  ~40% wins is break-even (60-70% then is very good); 80% required win rate = too much pressure.
  Higher R → lower win rate "that's just the math". No one has a 100% win rate.
- Reversal trading would require more confirmation (double SMT asked; "if I'm trading a
  reversal, I want to have more confirmation of that").
- T-spot (indicator) prints only in the third and fourth candle, not after.
- Gray indicator boxes = where he determines what counts as a "small wick".
- **Learning drill used by his brother**: two TradingView indicator templates, "all" and
  "quiz" (indicator elements off); mark candle 2 closure, CISD, EQ etc. by hand on the quiz
  template, then switch to "all" to check. "He just reverse engineered a ton."
- Monthly: if NQ does not close over the level, read further downside towards the monthly
  open and a "reversal month" — "I treat it like a candle too".
- Doesn't use: break of structure, IFVG, silver bullet, quarterly theory, supply/demand,
  volume (only range). Not a 10 p.m. (TTrades "10 p.m. candle") trader.
