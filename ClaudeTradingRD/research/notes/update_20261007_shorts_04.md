## Breaking Down the Consolidation Reversal Weekly Profile (8nXRD4mm_-o, 58 s)

### What is taught
A bullish consolidation-reversal week, walked day by day:
- Wednesday prints a **bearish expansion candle**. He refuses to call Wednesday the weekly low
  on that candle: "let another day play out".
- Thursday forms a reversal / candle 2 closure, and it is an **ideal** C2 closure because it
  also forms a protected swing.
- Consolidation through Wednesday + Thursday reversal = consolidation reversal profile, so
  Friday should expand.
- Confirmation on the hourly: from Thursday's low, has price closed through the series of
  down-close candles (CISD)? Yes. Then a **new continuation** forms. Friday expands higher off
  Thursday's reversal.

### Worked example
Unnamed instrument and week. Wed bearish expansion, Thu ideal bullish C2, hourly CISD from
Thursday's low, new continuation, Friday expansion up. No levels given.

### Hygiene
Clean. "out of this cap" probably = "out of this gap" (caption error, context unclear).
Calling a week "consolidation through Wednesday" when Wednesday is itself an expansion candle
is not reconciled.

## What Makes an Ideal C2 Closure? (EbH-sjWak74, 48 s)

### What is taught
Bearish case. Price sweeps a small high at a point of interest and prints a candle 2 closure.
It is ideal because (a) price is at a POI, (b) the closure closes through the series of
up-close candles — **"in this case, it's just one"** — so it forms a protected swing together
with the C2 closure. Marks: the opening price and the EQ; the next high should form in that
area as a **shallow high**, then trade lower. Example retests the level and trades lower.

### Hygiene
Timeframe not stated. "The opening price" is ambiguous (series open vs new candle open).

## Letting the Wick and Intracandle CISD Form (L_uhgevnHJA, 55 s)

### What is taught
Bearish, hourly chart aligned to a daily candle. Prior candle = candle 2 closure ("candle to
closure" in captions) with a valid CISD; a new continuation formed **before the new candle
opens**, so that high should hold. Inside the new candle price chops up into a point of
interest and closes through the series of up-close candles there = valid intracandle CISD.
That protected high is treated as the daily wick high, which must stay intact. Expansion lower
follows. Lesson: let the wick form on the continuation candle, let the IC-CISD form, then trade.

### Hygiene
"candle to closure" = candle 2 closure. Two different protective highs are named; which one
anchors the stop is not said.

## Learn To Be Uncomfortable (WiS6W0I-rUI, 57 s)

### What is taught (psychology, Q&A answer)
Question: "How to fix early exit. Do you trail stop loss?" His diagnosis: being stopped out
does not hurt much; what hurts is a trade that runs toward TP and comes back against you. The
urge to trail the stop fast is just letting the fear remain. You control entry, stop and TP;
you have "absolutely zero control" over what happens in between. Fix: place the trade and learn
to be uncomfortable — don't adjust over the fear. Untestable.

## Just Because a Weekly Profile Forms, Doesn't Mean It Will Play Out (zxglNIJSIZo, 60 s)

### What is taught
A **failed** Thursday-counter. Mon/Tue/Wed expand away from the weekly open (up). Expectation:
Thursday counters, Friday continues lower to the weekly open. Ideal requirement: the daily wick
forms "in this lower half in this T spot" — open, form a high, then an intraday continuation
lower. Actual: price grinds up, starts lower, reaches into an intraday FVG and **expands through
it** — "not respecting this area… more so consolidating" — then respects an inversion and trades
through the high. Lesson: a forming weekly profile is "not 100%. Things do fail."

### Hygiene
"Lower half" for a bearish wick high reads backwards (would expect upper half/premium); the
reference range is unstated. Whether the failed day is Thursday or Friday is unclear.
