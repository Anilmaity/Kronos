## New York Open Live Q&A  (wa9m30UHrm0, https://youtu.be/wa9m30UHrm0, 1822s)

### What is actually taught
- **Relative-strength target transfer (the reason for the stream).** Bearish index day. YM had
  already expanded, hit its target and was consolidating, so he labels it the *stronger* asset;
  NQ, with the most expansion beyond its previous day low, is the *weakest* and is "dying to catch
  up". Rule as stated: "you can use the strongest one to consider a target on the not strongest
  one" — watch YM for its previous day low; YM tagging its PDL is the exit on the NQ short. NQ
  retraced from there. Later in the stream the ranking flips: NQ hit its target and became the
  stronger, YM the weakest — so he would no longer short NQ.
- **Confirming the high of the day (NQ).** Two alternatives, "either ... or": an hourly CISD
  (hourly consolidation, sweep of its high, hourly close through the up-close candles that made
  the high) — "from 6:00 a.m. and on, you expect this high to be in"; or a 4-hour candle closure,
  ideally a candle 3 closure. Execution: 4-hour / 15-minute model, a small SMT on the previous high,
  entry on the 15-minute protected swing, target 2R "for these previous lows" or until YM hits its
  target. He says many Discord members took it.
- **Pre-open timeframe floor (personal rule).** Before 9:30 he trades only the 15-minute or higher
  ("keeps me from getting chopped up prior to the open"); after the open any timeframe.
- **Failure to manipulate -> trend day.** NQ/YM traded through the previous day low and formed a
  continuation rather than a reversal (no move back into the range, no bullish CISD). That marks a
  trending, "bloody" day; "Don't try to catch lows on a trend day". 10:00 a.m. open is "either
  bearish or nothing". Cascade read on the daily: YM takes its low without manipulating -> ES to its
  low -> NQ heading for its lows. Ties to "fighting the daily candle" from the previous stream.
- **News.** Only FOMC, NFP, CPI — "Don't trade right before it. Otherwise, I do not care." Orange
  folder ignored. Secondary: crude oil inventories (normally 10:30) only if trading oil; bond
  auctions sometimes; the Fed chair speaking.
- **Bank holidays:** he does not trade them.
- **Viewer who blew an account** shorting NQ on 15m/1m/5m/15s: too-low timeframes from fear of
  missing the move; use the higher timeframe — the trade was "quite straightforward on the
  15-minute"; higher timeframe means fewer stop-outs, not a missed move.
- Misc: does not use standard-deviation projections; does not watch 78.5 (Fib) or RTY ("same as
  YM"); practise phases of price by watching price around a previous day high/low; mentorship due
  October 1st; indicator can send alerts.

### Worked examples (levels/times as stated)
- NQ short: high confirmed by hourly CISD (~6:00 a.m.) or 4H C3 closure; 15m protected-swing entry;
  2R / previous lows; exit cue YM reaching its previous day low. No prices spoken.
- Gold (tweeted 10:30 p.m. the night before): "two daily highs or no ... if yes, low of day should
  be in". Bullish closure; wanted the intraday EQ to hold; a low below EQ was swept = intraday CISD,
  V-shape reversal, target previous day high (hit), move to break-even. Then a bearish CISD at PDH and
  failure — an invalidation trade whose draw becomes the range low; now "sketchy", wants the daily
  close before deciding. Still long-term bullish.
- EURUSD: range-bound, declines to trade; would accept a reversal higher if the dollar runs its
  low/high appropriately; wants the day back toward the daily open.
- Crude/energy: bullish, 3 days expansion, wants a retracement for another continuation.
- Crypto: daily swing point CISD with a candle 2 -> short into the lows.
