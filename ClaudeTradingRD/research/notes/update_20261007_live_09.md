## New York Open Live Q&A  (Hlwq1dRjBZo, https://youtu.be/Hlwq1dRjBZo, 4023s) — voice: ttrades

Solo TTrades stream, no guest. A losing/no-trade day that he explicitly frames as a lesson day. A good
portion of the runtime is giveaway admin (entry code "taco", ttrades.com/enter form, IP/email
de-duplication) and chat banter — no trading content there.

### Pre-open analysis (as stated)
- **Indices: bullish.** Reason: an SMT off a low ("SMT off this low ... taking out that low"); in the
  closing recap he adds that ES reversed off the low with an **hourly CISD**, so "from my intraday
  reversal video, I'm expecting this to be the low of the day". Plan = look for a higher low /
  continuation to frame higher.
- **But not an expansion day:** "we have a larger EQ which means it's not set up for expansion" — the
  EQ was not respected, i.e. a large opposing run. With a large opposing run his targets shrink to:
  **daily open → overnight lows or intraday liquidity → potentially the previous day high.** His own
  practical target: take out the Asia highs.
- **Gold:** consolidation; silver has taken a high, so a silver reversal off it could produce a gold
  reversal (SMT), favouring "consolidation profile around the daily open, or more bearish". He refuses to
  dwell on gold ("gold could be consolidating for two years").
- **Oil:** HTF consolidation, not touched (later: bearish/cool-off idea, will not long highs). **Dollar:**
  bullish into the highs of the range, so foreign currencies bearish.
- **The only idea of the day: NQ to the overnight highs.** Requirements: a run/sweep off the open, then a
  reversal, then a continuation.

### Opening rules stated
- **No trading through the open today.** Price had taken out a short-term high (not the bigger one), and
  "when we have some protected swings lined up after taking out something, a lot of times we go run out
  that protected swing before we actually get the continuation". Chat lesson: you would not long the open
  "because you have no swings you're expecting to hold after we take out a high".
- "I can't trade higher from failure swings like this" — needs something to run out the lows first.
- Possibly wait until 10:00 to see a V-shape and a decent closure.

### Live sequence (NQ strongest, ES middle, YM weakest — read live at the open)
1. YM runs out its lows (weakest asset). He wants YM to **reverse** there — "ideally you want the weakest
   asset to also reverse to help this one" — and the focus stays NQ to the overnight highs.
2. He prefers that YM makes **another sweep** before reversing: it "catches anyone else out that is short
   or early on into the longs and then it provides another SMT". But he does **not** want YM to go all the
   way to the deeper low — too much time and range consumed.
3. Waits for the 15-minute close (9:45) and then plans to drop to the 1-minute for a continuation. "Waiting
   for the closure ... keeps you from dumb positions."
4. **Declines entering on the 15-minute C2** because it has a large wick: large wick → targets are the daily
   open / intracandle highs, and he expects "some sort of retracement and then the following candle to do
   the expansion".
5. Notes a FVG in the upper half of the wick — acceptable for price to reach into it, "that's where we need
   to form a higher low". Protected swing marked by the indicator; ideal re-entry at 50% of that leg.
6. YM keeps going lower instead of reversing; NQ sweeps a short-term high and stalls. "When these two assets
   are not aligning, it makes it quite difficult to go anywhere" → consolidation. "One of them is lying" —
   ideally YM is the liar; NQ turned out to be the liar.
7. 4H read: for a candle-3 continuation "you want to see it respect the EQ" — price was sitting right at the
   EQ, so it needed a better closure.
8. Repeated "failure swing, failure swing ... gap" — the deep run left failure swings, so he cannot trust the
   low; he contrasts a low he can trust with one he cannot. Last idea: run the consolidation into a 1-minute
   gap, V-shape, then continuation. It fails ("Idea is wrong").
9. **Positional entry declined at 10:00.** Definition given: "a positional entry is a protected swings prior to
   the open of the new higher time frame candle". Whether to take one depends on confidence in the bias and
   that the low holds; with low confidence he would first let the 4-hour candle form its wick.
10. Trade management Q: a positional reversal entry at the end of the previous candle would have been stopped,
    or you "just scratch it" when the other assets continue against you.
11. Late YM continuation: he does not trade that late in the session; the daily profile has used so much time
    and range.
12. Expansion timing: "If price is going to expand, it's not going to wait ... 20 minutes to go expand".

**Outcome: no trade, idea wrong (NQ to overnight highs failed).** Recap: hourly CISD off the ES low → expected
low of day → wanted a higher low / continuation → no continuation formed, assets misaligned, consolidation.
If bearish instead, he would have needed NQ (strongest) to reverse first, then short ES.

### Lessons of the day (his own list)
1. "If bullish, use the weakest asset reversing to frame longs [on] the strongest. If bearish, use the
   strongest asset [re]versing to frame shorts on the weakest."
2. "Continuation will save you from reversal losses" — when wrong on bias, waiting for clean continuations
   keeps you out.

### Other Q&A rules stated
- **News:** "I avoid the day of CPI and NFP ... FOMC"; day before FOMC is fine; denies ever saying to avoid the
  session prior to PPI.
- **Chart data:** back-adjust ON (so the chart aligns with the ETF/QQQ and the index NDX — the rollover gap is
  not real); settlement OFF ("I want the actual close").
- **SMT vs relative strength:** "I use more of the relative strength". "If I'm longing, I want to focus on the
  strongest", using the weakest only for timing. Does not use the "first presented fair value gap"; 7-hour
  profile "not really"; VIX "sometimes, not really".
- **Range/expansion:** price either ranges or expands; after an expansion, wait for the range before trading.
- **Daily bias practice:** go to the daily chart, practise daily high/low, previous day's EQ and the daily profile.
  He starts every day on the daily chart. Recommends his intraday reversal video ("best video on the channel").
- **Psychology:** with a 50% win rate you will lose three in a row; you need ~100 trades to know your win rate.
  "Nothing works 100% of the time." Goal of a session is to learn, not to be right. Profitability claims need
  3-6 months.
- Upcoming content mentioned: dealing ranges video this week, ADR next week; course out October 1st.
