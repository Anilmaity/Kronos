## New York Open Live Q&A  (4rNC3QXxC20, https://youtu.be/4rNC3QXxC20, 5591s)

### What is actually taught

**Opening read (about 09:30).** Gold and silver bullish ("bullish continuation", captioned as
"Builder continuation"); oil bullish, hit his earlier target, next looking for the 90s then 100;
dollar consolidating. Indices: higher timeframe not good for longs - price closed back inside
the previous range high, several failure swings, a possible return toward the monthly open.
The day is an **inside day**, so the only reference points are previous day high, previous day
low and the EQ, and a reaction at the EQ opens a move lower. NQ is the weakest of the three.
Daily profile: no established low formed off the open (only a failure swing to the low), so he
cannot be bullish; bias for the session is bearish NQ.

**EQ close is a "soft invalidation", not a bias switch (repeated four times).** He took a
loss on an NQ long about 10 minutes in. Viewers who went long because price closed over the
daily EQ are corrected: a close over the EQ means the candle has gone too high to form a
small wick and expand; it now has a large wick. He keeps the bearish bias and moves targets to
the daily open, the liquidity around the open, and intraday lows. Bias only flips when the EQ
break is paired with opposing structure and an opposing daily profile. He shows his website's
"reversal candle" graphic: large wick + small body, large opposing run, price likely to close
near its open, targets = opening price and liquidity around the open. This contradicts library
rules saying an EQ close flips the bias (`invalidation-trade`); recorded as contested.

**Target choice.** Between the protected-swing low of the consolidation ("harder to get to")
and the low left of the V-shape ("the easy low"), he targets the easy low. NQ level marked at
stream start read as **29262.5** (caption; could be mistranscribed). NQ moved roughly 150-170
points to it. ES weaker in response, into a gap.

**Relative strength.** He never trades the lagging asset (YM, the strongest, while bearish);
the middle asset is acceptable. ES can stay valid as a continuation while reaching quite high,
because NQ is already far lower. At 10:00 (new 4H/1H/15m/5m/3m/1m candles) after a very
bearish candle hit the target: either done, or previous day low next; there is no swing to
trade from, so a further NQ short would need YM or ES to run a previous high (SMT) while NQ
does not - he describes this but is "not a personal fan" of later continuations; he likes to
be in before the expansion. Later "strength switched, now ES is in the middle" - expects NQ to
consolidate or drift while ES moves. When to trade ES instead of NQ: NQ already went through
the low, YM reversed off a high, ES has not reached its low - then trade ES to that low.

**SMT as candle 2.** His indicator marked nothing because mechanically the model needed a sweep
of the high; with SMT present he still treats it as a candle 2 reversal.

**TGIF rejected.** Checklist done on screen: Monday/Tuesday high - yes; expansion through
Thursday - Tuesday expansion, Wednesday consolidation, Thursday expansion; he corrects himself
to "three days of expansion, multiple days of expansion"; weekly objective - arguable. Closing
review: "It's not TGIF because we have this consolidation." Even when TGIF qualifies he wants
LTF alignment: normally a sweep of the previous day low traded back in, with SMT. Here the only
way in would have been overnight assuming the low of the week had formed, which he did not trust.

**Gold.** He refuses intraday gold shorts: do not fade breakouts, trade the trend, wait for a
swing low and trade it higher. The gold daily low is a protected low that is also the candle
closure (his "ideal formation" graphic); he wants 50% of that candle and the daily EQ respected.
Next day on the hourly a continuation formed, failed, the marked level held, candle 2 closure
expanded, then a consolidation breakout he calls less ideal (he traded it).

**1-minute live read (late session).** A reversal candle with a small wick that respects its EQ
supports expansion lower; with a large wick, wait for the expansion candle. Price hit the EQ,
respected it, closed below - new protected swing, broke the low. "If you saw this on a daily
chart" - same read, fractal. Then expansion, small consolidation candle; normally wants a small
sweep before the next leg; in expansions he does not want price to retrace up "to a discount"
(he states he uses premium/discount the opposite way from ICT). The move then ripped against
him: "You're not always going to be right."

**Scalp habit.** When he anticipates a move and sees a sweep he sometimes "slaps it" at market with
a tiny stop; yesterday's instance ran about 6R and then stopped out at break even.

**Protected-swing failure.** A protected swing usually fails and gets re-swept before continuing
after significant liquidity has been taken (price reaches toward the final target, fails,
re-sweeps, goes). He admits he cannot yet explain it mechanically; he does not use "double purge".

**Viewer trade (IC-CISD London long on a C2 to the daily EQ).** He approves: inside a range,
target the EQ or the range; 15-minute candle closure, stop on the protected swing. Overnight he
uses the 15-minute because lower timeframes are sloppy.

**Timeframe allocation.** 9-to-5 job: daily/hourly fractal. Off work: 4-hour/15-minute. On 4H/15m
you rarely miss moves - "only miss moves if you don't trade C2 in New York". The fractal model works
on stocks but he uses it there only on daily and monthly. A 3-minute trader should not care about
a monthly bias; HTF opinion matters only when the entry timeframe is high (4-hour).

**Execution / management.** Pre-open trades only with bias + aligned daily profile + an invalidation
he trusts not to be swept by 09:30 volatility. Swing trades: enter, set stop, go to bed. Beginners:
strict TP and SL, no management (tweet: a viewer would have had +6R extra by taking only 2R / -1R).
He teaches 2R "because it helps people with stats" but holds higher R himself. Repeatedly stopped out
on the 5-minute -> enter on the 15-minute. Would not short a missed NQ move after it hit target -
wants to short into the target. Ignores yellow-folder news (09:45 PMI). A stop hit means you were wrong.

**Tools / naming.** Uses FVGs, highs, lows, order blocks only; does not use OTE, IFVG, GEX, "TCISD"
(he never named that), quarter SMT. TTFM indicator: default settings; CISD shown (not solid) before the
candle closure and solid on closure; T-spot plotted; grey area = where the HTF wick should form.
Consolidation has no mechanical definition: "a high and a low that price stays within for a period of time".

**Psychology / career.** Four years of study is enough - apply it; the loss -> oversize -> tilt ->
blow account -> "learn more ICT" cycle; one strategy; ignore social media highlights. Strategy built on
swing points so no alpha decay. Backtesting is to learn what you want to see; asking an AI to backtest
his model is pointless; he no longer backtests. Root-cause triage for a stuck viewer: strategy issue,
tilt, or personal non-compliance.
