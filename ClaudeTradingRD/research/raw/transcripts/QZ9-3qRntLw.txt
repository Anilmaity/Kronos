A protected swing is the formation of new opposing candles or new order blocks. So, there are a few ways this can be made, so let's take a look at it. The first will be a sweep of a higher low. In this case, sweeping out a low in a bullish scenario. Once price closes through the series of down close candles that swept out that low, then this low is now protected and I can anticipate it to trade higher. Now, the only time price does not need to sweep out the low is when a correlated asset sweeps out the low and we form SMT. With the SMT, once price closes over the series of down close candles that made the failure swing, then it is protected and I can anticipate it to trade away. The other way a protected swing forms is out of a fair value gap. So, it doesn't need to sweep out a low, but it needs to reach into a fair value gap and then closing over the down close candles into that fair value gap protects this low and I anticipate it to trade away.
