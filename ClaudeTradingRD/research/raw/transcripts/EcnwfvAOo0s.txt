So what is a breaker block? A breaker block is a candlestick pattern that shows a reversal. Now this does need to be used with a higher timeframe bias, so don't pattern trade it. But what is it? A bearish breaker block will form a high, a low, a higher high, and a lower low. Then using the down close candles from the first high to the first low and marking that out, that will be our area of resistance for price to trade lower. For the bullish breaker block, it will be a low, a high, a lower low, and a higher high. Then I will look at the up close candles in the area between the first low and the first high. That will be the area of support for price to trade higher.
