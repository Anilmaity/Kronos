Let's take a look at my favorite reversal pattern. So, this is going to be over an order block or a change in the state of delivery. Here you can see we have a bullish trend and price is reaching into an important level. There are two requirements to look for this. I need price to reach into an important level and to sweep out a high to the left of it. When both of those are met, I'm going to find the series of upclose candles that swept out the high into that important level. Mark out the opening price in that series. And then when price closes below that level, I'm going to use that as the area of resistance for price to continue lower. In the bullish scenario, we need it to sweep out a low into an important level, then mark out the series of downclo candles. Once close over, that will be the area of support for price to trade higher.
