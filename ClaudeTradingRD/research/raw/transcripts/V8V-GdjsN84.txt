Once I have a daily bias, the open high, low, close of the daily candle is the most important thing. So, if I come into the day expecting price to trade higher, I know I want to let the daily candle form its lower wick. Because to trade higher, it needs to open, form a low before it can expand away to its high and close. So if I have an expansion candle that is going to have a small wick once it forms its change in the state of delivery, then I can trade the body of this candle higher to its average daily range. Now if the daily candle has a large wick where price opens, makes a large move down, I'm then going to want to target back towards the daily open or the current high of day because daily candles only have so much range.
