When you have expansion into a point of interest and expansion out, those two put together form a reversal. Now, it's important to understand that these reversals don't occur randomly, but at highs and lows or an important level. Now, if I want to trade these reversals, there's two ways I can go about it. I can look to trade the actual reversal point, in which case I'll use a lower time frame change in the state of delivery at that reversal point. Or I can look to trade the continuation after the reversal has already confirmed and started to trade away.
