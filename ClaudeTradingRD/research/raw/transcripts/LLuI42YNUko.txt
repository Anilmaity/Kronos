How do you use projections? With projections, you are going to find the reversal point, and once there is a change in the state of delivery, you're going to find the manipulation leg. You're going to do that by finding the low, then finding the high that made the low, and then using a Fibonacci to mark out those as anchor points to project out targets. That is where you're going to look for this bullish move to continue to. Now, in the bearish scenario, you're going to do the same thing, but you're going to anchor from the high to the low, and then look for the bearish move to reach for those projections.
