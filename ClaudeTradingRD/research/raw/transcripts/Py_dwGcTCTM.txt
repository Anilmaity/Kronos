Why are you always getting caught in a consolidation? Well, this doesn't have to do with the lower time frame, it has to do with the higher time frame. So, taking a look here, many people will see this trend starting and then look to chase it below some lows or just chase it in general. What happens a lot is you're entering at the end of a higher time frame expansion candle. If you're going to want to get on side with this move, stop entering at the end of higher time frame candles, wait for the new one to open, form its wick, and then look to trade it lower.
