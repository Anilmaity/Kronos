Consolidation is when price stays within a given range for a period of time. When do I generally expect consolidation to occur? Well, that is following a large range or after price reaches an important level. How do I interact with a consolidation? If I look to trade it, well, I'm going to wait for the either the high or the low of the range to get taken out. If the low of the range gets taken out first, that is generally bullish as I want to trade to the other side of the range. If the high of the range gets taken out first, that is generally bearish as I want to trade to the low of the range. Now, the only time the range doesn't have to get taken out is if a correlated asset does take it out or creates SMT and then I can trade to the other side of the range without having a manipulation.
