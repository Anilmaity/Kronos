Let's talk about how to spot a broading formation. You can see we have some sort of range and then as we move forward, price takes out one side of the range and then takes out the other side of the range. It continues to fail to break out and then trade back through the range once again. So if it's failing this side, where is it likely to go? Back through the range again. If it fails this side, it'll go through the range again. And then drawing out the pattern, you can see how it looks like that. So the way I found most people succeed in drawing these correctly is drawing out buyside and sellside liquidity. When we take one side of the range, what you do is extend those lines out. So this low here took out sellside liquidity. I would connect those lines together and then extend it. This high here took out buyside liquidity. So I'd connect those two together and extend it.
