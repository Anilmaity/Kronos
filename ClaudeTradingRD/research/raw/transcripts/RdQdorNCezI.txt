A retracement is a slow shallow move opposing the higher time frame trend. So when we have expansion and a slow shallow move into a PDA, normally a fair value gap, that can be considered a retracement and then I can look for price to expand again from that area. If I'm looking to get on side with a retracement, I want to wait until that retracement phase is over and then get on side during or prior to the expansion. So you can see here when we retrace into that fair value gap, I can then go into the lower time frames, look for a change in the state of delivery, and then get on side with that reversal or the continuation that follows.
