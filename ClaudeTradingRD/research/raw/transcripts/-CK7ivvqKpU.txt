Top-down analysis is when I combine multiple time frames in the same direction to increase my probability of a trade working out. So you can see here I first want to identify a bias. That is where I choose a direction on the day. So on my daily chart, is it bullish? Is it bearish? Is it likely to expand lower or expand higher? Once I have identified that, in this case, I'm anticipating price to expand lower. I then want to go down to my hourly chart to look for structure that aligns with that. I want to wait for the hourly chart to become bearish. If it is still bullish, I wait for it to be bearish. Once I have a change in the state of delivery in a point of interest, then I can go look for an entry on my 5-minut time frame. And what you'll notice is all of these time frames are pointing down or bearish. This is then when expansion occurs.
