How do I use each of these time levels? With the 8:30 and midnight open, I will consider premium or discount as well as support and resistance. So, for instance, if I am bearish here and we are above both 8:30 and midnight, then that is a deep premium and vice versa. Now, with Judith swing or an order block, I'll use midnight, 8:30, and 9:30. And what I'm looking for there is if I'm bullish, I want to see opposing runs at these times. So for instance bullish here 8:30 makes an opposing run closes over that's now an order block. I don't expect this low to be taken as we continue higher. Similar thing here. 9:30 makes an opposing run lower to continue higher.
