If I'm looking to trade a reversal at the 9:30 open, there is a particular sequence I want to see. I want to see the higher time frame candle have a small wick and then 9:30 create a change in the state of delivery on the lower time frame. This can be formed as a closure right at 9:30 open or it can make an opposing run and then close over. Following this change in the state of delivery on the lower time frame, I'm then going to be looking for 930 to10 to expand higher to fill out this expansion candle on the hourly or 30 minute chart.
