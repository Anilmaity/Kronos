Price is always going to form one of three things. It can form a continuation, a reversal, or a consolidation. Let's take a look at how you can determine which scenario will occur. So, here you can see we have a candle's range and price closes outside the range. With price closing outside, I can anticipate a continuation in the following candle. Now, in the same scenario, price reaches above its previous candle's high, but fails to close outside the range, closing back inside the range. With this, I can anticipate a reversal or for price to continue lower. Now, if price takes out the high and the low of the range and closes back in, that is where I can anticipate a consolidation to occur.
