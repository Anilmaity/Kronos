So what is the London reversal daily profile? The London reversal daily profile is when London forms a high or low of day. So in this bullish example, price opens, forms its low during London, has a change in the state of delivery, which confirms this higher time frame wick, and then as we get into New York, price expands. This is creating an expansion candle on the higher time frame or the daily chart.
