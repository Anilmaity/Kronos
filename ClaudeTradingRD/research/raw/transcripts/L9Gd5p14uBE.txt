Low resistance liquidity is just a failure swing. So how I like to picture it is we have liquidity resting above highs and lows. So as we trade away, we then generate more highs and lows and thus more liquidity. Well, high resistance liquidity is the opposite of low resistance liquidity. It is where we first sweep that liquidity before the reversal. And then what this does is wipes out the liquidity resting right here and right here. So now the liquidity is resting above this high and low. And that is what creates high resistance liquidity.
