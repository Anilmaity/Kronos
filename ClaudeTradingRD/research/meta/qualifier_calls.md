## Why the curated set is small
The regex surfaces hundreds of hits but almost all of them are the author *using* the word, not *adjudicating* with it. A usable label needs the author to look at one object and return a verdict, and that is rare: the corpus teaches by definition and demonstration, not by labelled examples. 56 genuinely-labelled calls is the honest yield. None of them can be tied to a datable bar (there is no chart in a transcript and no instrument or date is spoken), so they constrain **which quantity** to measure, not **what value** it takes. That distinction drives everything in `meta/threshold_fits.md`.
