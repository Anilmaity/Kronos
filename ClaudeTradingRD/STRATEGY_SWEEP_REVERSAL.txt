SWEEP-REVERSAL (MANIPULATION -> DISTRIBUTION) STRATEGY — FULL SPECIFICATION
================================================================================
Instrument : XAUUSD (research data: OANDA XAU_USD mid-price)
Timeframe  : 5-minute candles (resampled from OANDA M1)
Status     : research prototype, locked config as of 2026-07-07
Repo       : ClaudeTradingRD (commits b1ad9e9 .. 2f8e4a2)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CONCEPT (user's thesis, statistically confirmed)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before a real move the market makes a MANIPULATION move: it breaks a top /
bottom / liquidity level (PDH/PDL, session high/low, swing point) to take the
stops, then reverses and runs the OTHER direction (distribution) toward the
nearest opposite liquidity. Measured facts (3mo XAU 1m + 18mo BTC 1m):
  - 75-77% of liquidity-level breaks fail and reverse (manipulation rate)
  - distribution runs median ~1.1x the manipulation leg; >=2.5x only ~21%
  - 63-66% of reversals reach the nearest opposite liquidity level within 4h
  - manipulation is ~20x faster (price/bar) than the distribution that follows

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. LEVEL & SWEEP DETECTION (backtest_sweep_reversal.py::detect_sweeps)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Liquidity levels tracked on the trading TF (5m):
  PD      : prior-day high & low (UTC day), replaced at each day rollover
  session : completed-session highs/lows; sessions (UTC): asia 00-07,
            london 07-12, ny 12-21
  swing   : fractal swing highs/lows, half-width K=10 bars, become active
            K bars after the pivot (no lookahead)
  Level lifetime: swing/session expire after 1440 bars; consumed when swept.

Sweep state machine per level:
  break     : bar's high crosses above a 'high' level (or low below a 'low')
  extreme   : max/min excursion while beyond the level
  CONFIRMED : a bar CLOSES back through the level within CONFIRM_N=15 bars
              of the break -> this is a manipulation signal
  breakout  : no close-back within 15 bars -> genuine break, level dropped
  One signal per confirmation bar: deepest penetration wins.
  Signal fields: direction (down if a high was swept / up if a low),
  level, extreme, m_pen = |extreme - level|, manip_bars = break->confirm,
  target = nearest opposite untouched level at confirmation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. VALIDITY FILTER (the locked one) — HTF15 "contained-or-wick"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A signal is VALID if EITHER (both lookahead-safe):
  contained : the whole manipulation (break bar .. confirmation bar) falls
              inside ONE 15-minute candle (epoch-aligned bucket), i.e. the
              sweep prints as a wick on the 15m chart, OR
  wick      : the previous COMPLETED 15m candle swept the level and closed
              back through it (high>level & close<level for down signals).
Implementation: backtest_compare.py::htf_masks(df, sweeps, 15) -> (c15, w15);
valid = c15 | w15.   (User's rationale: real manipulations appear as wicks
sweeping levels on 15M/30M.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TRADE RULES (locked config "HTF15 cont|wick + 2xBE")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Signals   : ALL valid confirmations, overlapping trades allowed
              (no one-position constraint, no fast/size filters)
  Entry     : LIMIT order at the swept level (the retest), working for
              ENTRY_TTL=30 bars after confirmation; if price path would hit
              the stop on the same touch bar, trade is void (conservative)
  Stop      : beyond the sweep extreme + 10% of penetration
              (short: stop = extreme + 0.1*m_pen)
  Target    : 2.0 x m_pen from entry, in the reversal direction
  Min RR    : skip if |TP-entry| / |stop-entry| < 1.0
  BE move   : once price moves +1R in favour, stop -> entry (scratch)
  Time exit : close position at market after HORIZON=240 bars (20h on 5m)
  Ties      : if SL and TP touched in the same bar, count SL (conservative)
  Cost      : $0.30 round trip subtracted from every trade's PnL
  SIZING    : MUST be risk-normalized (equal $ risk per trade, lots =
              risk$/(|stop-entry|*100) rounded to 0.01). Fixed-lot sizing
              DESTROYS the edge (verified: fixed 0.01 lot was net negative).

6. WHAT WAS TESTED AND REJECTED (do not re-add blindly)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - 1m trading TF: spread ($0.30) = ~0.17R per trade on $1.80 stops; every
    config with WR>=45% was net negative; maxDD -75R. 5m fixes this.
  - Fixed-multiple TP without BE on 1m: negative in all variants.
  - Nearest-liquidity TP (no BE): +78R/3mo on 1m but 17% WR, top-10 trades
    = ALL the profit, 25-loss streaks, monthly instability. Runner-dependent.
  - Killzone timing as HARD filter: London-open 07-10 UTC ~flat; NY-close
    19-21 UTC NEGATIVE (-0.34 avgR); NY-open 12-15 UTC is the only window
    beating baseline quality (avgR +0.17, PF 1.37) but timing filters keep
    only ~15-20% of total profit. Use as size/skip modifier, not a gate.
    (Hour effects were the least stable stat across datasets — low trust.)
  - FAST (<=3 bar) + size-floor filters: efficiency only (fewer trades,
    similar netR); superseded by the HTF15 filter which actually raises avgR.
  - RAW 2xBE baseline (no validity filter): +67.8R but avgR only +0.14 and
    5.3 trades/day — kept as benchmark row, not the locked config.
